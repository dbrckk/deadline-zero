Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Deadline: Zero is designed to work without creating a user account and without sending gameplay records to a developer-operated backend.

Data handled by the app

The current Godot release candidate stores only local app settings on the device, such as audio and comfort preferences.

The current release does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information, gameplay telemetry, advertising identifiers, purchase records, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, health information, or account credentials to the developer.

Third-party SDKs

The current Godot release candidate does not include advertising, analytics, attribution, Play Billing, Play Games Services, cloud-save, account/login, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Google Play and Android may process information independently when the app is downloaded, updated, backed up, or used on an Android device. Those platform activities are governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policies.

Local storage

Audio and comfort settings are stored locally so they can persist between app sessions. Deleting the app or clearing its app data may remove those settings, subject to Android behavior and backup configuration.

Children

The app does not provide a developer-operated account system or knowingly collect personal information from children. Google Play target-audience and content-rating declarations are managed separately for each release.

Changes to this policy

If a future release adds analytics, advertising, purchases, online accounts, cloud services, diagnostics, or any feature that changes data handling, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before publication.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing or the project issue tracker.

Release consistency

This policy describes the current Godot shipping candidate. It must not be reused unchanged for a future build whose data flows differ.
