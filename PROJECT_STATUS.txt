IMPORTANT DECISIONS
-------------------
- Do not spend generation credits forcing exact reference poses when deterministic rig/render tooling can solve the production requirement.
- Do not destructively modify immutable source assets.
- Never accept an actor solely because CI is green; phone-scale rendered QA and real Android gameplay evidence remain mandatory.
- Production atlas publication must be additive/upsert-based; publishing one actor must never erase another actor page.
- Source assets must be license-identified and fingerprinted before entering production.
- Prefer native authored actions from source masters; procedural motion remains a fallback, not the quality target.
- The shared 8-direction/232-frame/96px contract is now configuration-driven and must remain stable unless intentionally versioned.
