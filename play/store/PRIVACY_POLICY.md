# Deadline: Zero — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 5, 2026

Deadline: Zero is designed to work without creating a user account and without sending gameplay records to a developer-operated backend.

## Data handled by the app

The current Godot release candidate stores only local app settings on the device, such as audio and comfort preferences.

The current release does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information, gameplay telemetry, advertising identifiers, purchase records, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, health information, or account credentials to the developer.

## Third-party SDKs

The current Godot release candidate does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, attribution SDKs, Play Billing, Play Games Services, cloud save, account/login SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Google Play and the Android operating system may process information independently when the app is downloaded, updated, backed up, or used on an Android device. Those platform-level processing activities are governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policies.

## Local storage

Audio and comfort settings are stored locally so they can persist between app sessions.

Deleting the app or clearing its app data may remove those settings, subject to Android device behavior and backup configuration.

## Children

The app does not provide a developer-operated account system or knowingly collect personal information from children.

The Google Play target-audience and content-rating declarations for each release are managed separately in Play Console and must match the exact published build.

## Changes to this policy

If a future release adds analytics, advertising, purchases, online accounts, cloud services, diagnostics, or any other feature that changes data handling, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before that release is published.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing or the project issue tracker:

https://github.com/dbrckk/deadline-zero/issues

## Release consistency rule

This policy describes the current Godot shipping candidate. It must not be reused unchanged for a future build whose data flows differ.
